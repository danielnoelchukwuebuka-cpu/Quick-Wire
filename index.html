How does the demo estimate work?

Is my information sent anywhere?

Can I use this for a real transfer?

Who is Quickwire for?

Anyone who wants to preview a guided international transfer flow.

QUICKWIRE is a clickable prototype. No money movement, exchange-rate quote, bank connection, identity verification, or regulatory approval is available in this experience.
